# Overloading

A name, an operator, or an initialization can call more than one
function. The pages in this section give the rules for:

- the implicit conversion sequence of each argument,
- the choice of one function from the candidates.

These pages use name lookup from the foundations, and the constructors,
conversion functions, and operator functions of the classes section.
The templates section adds function templates to the candidates.
