# Templates

A template is a declaration with parameters. Each list of arguments for
the parameters names one specialization: a class, a function, a
variable, or a type. The pages in this section give the rules for:

- declaring templates and their parameters,
- writing template arguments and matching them to parameters,
- explicit and partial specializations, and how a specialization is
  selected.

templates, dependent names, instantiation, constraints, and packs
beyond their declaration are not specified yet
