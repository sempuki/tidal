# Q-0186: May a proposal rest on judgments from outside Tidal?

**Status:** provisional
**Affects:** `documentation/exercises.md` (Propose)
**Waits for:** the maintainer (pending.md row 9)

## Context

Proposal X4 judged how often its affected patterns occur in real
programs. Tidal gives no frequency data, so the judgments came from
the reader's general experience. Propose readers take all their C++
knowledge from Tidal (decision 0037).

## Evidence

Finding X4-14 in `reports/clean-room/X4/report.md`.

## Options

1. A proposal states such judgments only when it marks them as outside
   knowledge.
2. A proposal states no judgment that Tidal cannot support.

## Provisional answer

Yes, marked as outside knowledge (option 1). The judgments matter to a proposal's case, and the mark
keeps them apart from what Tidal states.

Kept open on 2026-11-03 under decision 0054, because the question waits on the maintainer (`documentation/decisions/pending.md`, row 9).
