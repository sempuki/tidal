# Tidal

Tidal is an open specification of a language that is compatible with
ISO C++23. The site follows the four kinds of documentation of the
[Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr/) framework. Each kind serves one need,
and the kinds stay apart.

| | For study | For work |
|---|---|---|
| **Practical steps** | **Tutorials** that teach by doing: {doc}`guides/reading-the-spec` | **How-to guides** for one task each: {doc}`guides/resource-owner`, {doc}`guides/error-handling`, {doc}`guides/safe-arithmetic`, {doc}`guides/enum-switch` |
| **Knowledge** | **Explanation** of why the language is as it is: none yet | **Reference** that states what the language does: the {doc}`specification <specification>`, its {doc}`glossary` and its {doc}`rule index <rules>` |

The specification is the reference, and only the reference. A tutorial,
a how-to guide, or an explanation does not belong in it. The guides cite
the rules for every claim and add no fact of their own. Where a guide
and a rule disagree, the rule decides.
