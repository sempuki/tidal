These choices hold until the maintainer decides otherwise:

- **One completion count.** `frontend_check.py` prints the count that
  decision 0060's first condition uses. Round reports and roadmaps
  copy that line.
- **A test that the front end leaves out has a row.** The row in the
  front-end ledger is `out of scope` and gives the reason, and the
  front-end check leaves the test out.
- **A first try is a program test.** A roadmap or Write program's
  first try that builds clean on GCC and Clang becomes a program test
  beside its last version.
- **A probe the front end accepts against two compilers is a reject
  test**, with its rule or a question.
- **A reader report lists its host-only claims.** A claim that rests on
  the host GCC and Clang alone is listed, so that a writer probes it on
  the matrix before acting.
- The choices of decisions 0051, 0054, 0055, 0056, 0057, 0058, and 0059
  stay.
