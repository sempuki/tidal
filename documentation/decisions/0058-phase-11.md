(`reports/clean-room/roadmap-8/`): a programmer, an implementer, an
editor, a clean-room writer, and a language designer. They agree on
these points:

  changed no rule's class.
- Phase 10 left findings with no record of an answer. A commit
  promised R10-1 to R10-7 and R10-9 to RV3's repairs, and no later

  program of G152, and they give the shape of `std::align_val_t` that
  PU3-4 asks for.
- TR-P1 and the TR-4 note disagree with TR-4 on a program with no

  RV3, I3, W18, PU3, and X22 repairs gave them. Q-0403 and Q-0411 have
  no review. 8 review-held questions rest on text that changed after
  their review.
- Records drift. 14 Divergences lines call a settled question open.
  The status page's front-end paragraph names a finished round.

- The front end runs 37 of 38 short programs of common forms. It stops

- I1, I2, and I3 each found more than a dozen missing steps. COND-16

These choices hold until the maintainer decides otherwise:

- **Every finding has a ledger row.** A commit that defers a finding
  adds a `planned` row that names the item. The closing commit of a
  phase lists the `planned` rows whose item ended without an answer.
- **A failed use names the principle.** When a principle use does not
  give the answer and the principle does not change in the same
  commit, the question's `**Waits for:**` line names `a principle
  (ID)`.
- **A packet by option each phase.** The closing commit of a phase
  gives the share of open questions that `pending.md` holds. When the
  share rises, the next phase's first items include a packet that the
  maintainer can answer row by row.
- **Check results come from a tool.** A commit's Checks paragraph is
  the output of the script that ran the checks.
- **Tools write the parts they can derive** (decision 0057) also covers
  the Divergences tags, the settlements table, the status page's
  front-end paragraph, and the done list of `exercises.md` section 6.
- The choices of decisions 0051, 0054, 0055, 0056, and 0057 stay.

A narrow second review of the questions held only by a review's doubt,
Diagnose batches, Write suite W20 on library-shaped code, Propose X24,
a shared impact scanner, a front-end prototype of a done proposal,
