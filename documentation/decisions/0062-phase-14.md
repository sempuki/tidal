These choices hold until the maintainer decides otherwise:

- **The gate for an early rewrite.** Decision 0060's "few" means at
  most 50 tests that fail their check, by the completion line of
  `tools/frontend_check.py`. "Stable over two rounds" means that no
  test moves from a passing status to a failing one in either round.
  When a front-end round ends inside the gate, and each remaining test
  has a named cause in the round's report, the next phase decision adds
  the rewrite as an item. The tests the rewrite takes over are named in
  that decision.
- **A repair diff gets a read.** A gap review's repair commit is read
  by a source reader before the item ends, and the read is recorded in
  `reports/review/`.
- **Every commit row names its commit**, in both ledgers.
- The choices of decisions 0051, 0054, 0055, 0056, 0057, 0058, 0059,
  and 0061 stay.

Propose X24 on P26, refresh notes for the done designs, a shared impact
scanner, pair exercise X14 with X23, an index of what a test of
validity can see, a narrow second review of the doubt-held questions,
interpreter output when every order agrees, first errors that name the
rule of the cause, and guides on storage, owners, and adaptors wait for
a later phase. Later versions stay out of scope until the maintainer
decides `pending.md` row 7.
