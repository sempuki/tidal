Date: 2026-11-04
Status: Provisional. Extends 0033, 0039, 0050, 0051, 0054, and 0055. All
items are done.

## Context

Phases 1 to 8 are done. Five clean-room views ranked what comes next
(`reports/clean-room/roadmap-6/`): a programmer, an implementer, an
editor, a clean-room writer, and a language designer. They agree on
these points:

- No topic needs a new page. Phase 8 added 5 rules and no page.
- Open questions went from 209 to 221 in phase 8. About 60 of the 221
  wait for a reader or reviewer to use a principle that item PQ
  widened. No rule says what counts as a use, and no exercise records
  one. The R8 readers named a principle 4 times in 982 answers.
- No review has read the text phase 8 wrote: 5 new rules, 111 rules
  and 66 principles whose prose changed, and Q-0377 to Q-0385.
- Front-end round 19 ended before most phase 8 repairs. 24 of the 84
  tests added after it fail on the front end, 4 of them against text
  that the front end's own findings changed. The programmer view found
  a false `undefined` verdict (VIRT-12 on a delete through a middle
  base class), four run stops, and two missed rejects in three
  programs. The front end accepts 6 page examples that the page marks
  as rejected, and no tool runs it on the examples. Findings G134 and
  G135 have no answer.
- W16 and W17 report no stop, and the interpreter stops on 2 of their 4
  programs. The Write prompt has no interpreter step.
- Records drift. `exercises.md` section 7 counts 18 pages with only
  named Predict batches, and 12 remain. Row 4 of `pending.md` is one
  cell of about 3400 characters. 36 open questions lack a dated reason
  in the common form. 17 `:tests:` entries name a test whose header does
  not cite the rule.
- 68 pages keep the older Evidence form.
- I1 found 13 gaps on a page that Predict and 17 front-end rounds had
  passed. Partial ordering and overload ranking are the next
  procedures for that kind of check.
- The Propose portfolio has one ready proposal left (P15).

## Decision

Phase 9 has these items. The roadmap's phase 9 table gives what each
item needs first.

| Item | Work | Source views |
|---|---|---|
| R3 | Close the phase 8 record: a definition of a use of a principle in the style guide, with a `principles` field in Predict answers that `tools/exercises.py score` counts; the count in `exercises.md` section 7 written by a tool; row 4 of `pending.md` as a section with one row per item; a dated reason for each open question that lacks one; `:tests:` entries that match their tests' headers, with a check; Q-0342; the interpreter result in the W16 and W17 rows | editor, writer, programmer |
| RS | Tests from the round 6 reductions, the correct whole sketches of rounds 4 to 6 as program tests, and LSUP-2 tests for the three ordering classes | programmer |

| PU | A principle-use batch: a reader applies the widened principles, without rule text, to the programs of the questions that wait on them; the writer settles each question the batch supports | writer, editor |
| FE | Front-end round 20: the phase 8 repair tests, the round 6 reductions, the false VIRT-12 verdict, the page examples, and the remaining EDG-AGREES tests | implementer, programmer, writer |
| V3 | The Evidence format on the other 68 pages, largest first, and a check that a converted page keeps the format | editor, writer |
| E | Implement I2 on partial ordering of function templates; Predict on the 12 pages with only named batches; Propose X21 on P15 | implementer, writer, editor, designer |

These choices hold until the maintainer decides otherwise:

- **A use of a principle.** A reader answer or a review reading uses a
  principle when its record names the principle and the question's
  program and reaches the answer by the principle's model. The
  question's dated note names the batch or review.
- **A front-end round follows the last exercise that adds tests.** A
  repair commit whose tests fail on the front end names the next round,
  and the status page lists those tests.
- **Exercise readers run the interpreter.** Write, Port, and Implement
  readers run their programs with `tidal-fe run` and reduce each stop.
- **The open-question aim is stated by cause.** The phase aims to end
  with fewer open questions than the 221 at its start, and its closing
  commit gives the change for each group: questions held for a
  principle use, questions in `pending.md`, and the rest.
- The choices of decisions 0051, 0054, and 0055 stay.

Port suites, guides on the site, Diagnose batches beyond D-classes, an
index of what a test of validity can see, a shared impact scanner, pair
exercises, new Propose proposals, and splitting the overloading
principles wait for a later phase. Later versions stay out of scope
until the maintainer decides `pending.md` row 7.
