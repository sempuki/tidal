# 0055. Phase 8: honest counts, principles that answer, and exercises by use

Date: 2026-11-03
Status: Provisional. Extends 0033, 0039, 0050, 0051, and 0054. All items
are done.

## Context

Phases 1 to 7 are done. Five clean-room views ranked what comes next
(`reports/clean-room/roadmap-5/`): a programmer, an implementer, an
editor, a clean-room writer, and a language designer. They agree on
these points:

- No topic needs a new page. Phase 7 added 13 rules and no page.
- The front-end counts claim more than they show. `conform` judges a
  test by its EDG override before its base expectation, so 99 tests
  pass by agreeing with EDG against the base. Against the base
  expectation alone, the check passes 6035 of 6337 tests, and the
  status page says 6134. The harness reads only the first explored run,
  so `subscript-multi-order.cpp` passes with a second outcome that
  OPR-7 forbids. Three runs stop at the bound of 64 and say nothing.
- Records drift again. `pending.md` row 1 counts 110 settled questions,
  and 124 settled under decision 0054. `question_index.py --check`
  warns on 49 questions with no review reading. `propose_check.py`
  reads X14's change table as 0 rows with no warning, and does not
  report a rule that changed after a report's commit. `exercises.md`
  sections 6 and 7, the roadmap's lanes, and the status page's size
  estimate describe finished or missing work.
- 53 to 59 open questions stay open because no principle on the page
  gives the answer. Phase 7 read that condition two ways. Nine
  questions settled on a principle that states only the general model,
  and ten stayed open because no principle states the case.
- No review has read the text that phase 7 wrote: 13 new rules, 7 class
  changes, the CLS-35 layout tables, and Q-0362 to Q-0376.
- 8 rules rest on one test, and 46 rest only on tests with an override.
  Rules that a review or question changes arrive with the one test that
  showed the split.
- Evidence took 60% of the page growth in phase 7, and much of it
  repeats the question files.
- Phase 7 ran no exercise by use. Three programs by the programmer view
  (405 lines) found a false `undefined` verdict (OBJ-2 on two objects in
  the rows of a byte array), a false reject (a member alias of a class
  template), and three interpreter stops. The specification's 181
  example blocks are not compiled by any tool, and the front end rejects
  the tuple protocol example of the language-support page.
- An implementer view finds that the front end learns from the tests as
  much as from the text. G123 to G127 found five gaps in CLS-35 and BF-9
  only because tests asserted sizes the table did not derive.

These choices hold until the maintainer decides otherwise:

- **No settling in the commit that changes the rule.** A question whose
  rule changed settles in a later commit, after a reader batch or a
  review has used the changed rule.
- **No settling on a new principle in the phase that writes it.** A
  question settles on a principle written in phase 8 only after a
  reader or reviewer has used that principle.
- **Each new question names its source.** A `**Source:**` line names
  the finding, review, or report.
- **Each phase runs at least one exercise by use.** Write, Port, or an
  Implement exercise counts.
- **Correct sketches become tests.** The commit that acts on a roadmap
  round adds each correct sketch and reduction to `tests/`.
- **A phase's closing commit updates its decision's status line.**
- The choices of decisions 0051 and 0054 stay.

Port suites, guides on the site, Diagnose batches beyond D-classes, an
index of what a test of validity can see, pair exercises, and a
construct count over `tests/programs/` wait for a later phase. Later
versions stay out of scope until the maintainer decides `pending.md`
row 7.

## Consequences

- `documentation/roadmap.md` gains a phase 8 table, and
  `documentation/status.md` points its next steps at it.
- The front-end pass counts fall when HN lands. The commit states the
  counts before and after.
- Each phase 8 commit that settles questions states the open count
  before and after. The phase aims to end with fewer open questions
  than the 209 at its start.
- The last step of the phase runs the full matrix with `--report`, a
  whole-repository similarity report, and `tools/status_table.py`,
  marks the roadmap rows, and updates this decision's status line.
