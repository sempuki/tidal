These choices hold until the maintainer decides otherwise:

- **Each phase names the review of its own text.** A phase decision
  names the gap review that reads the rules and principles that the
  previous phase changed. RV3 reads phase 9's text. Phase 11 reads
  phase 10's.
- **An open question states its current reason in one field.** The
  `**Waits for:**` header line names what holds the question: a use of
  a principle (by ID), a `pending.md` row, a review kind, or a split
  between newest families. The dated notes stay below as history.
  Tools select questions by the field, never by the wording of a note.
- **A repair restarts the wait only where it reaches the question.** The
  dated note of a repair names the question's programs that the changed
  principle decides differently. A change that decides none of them
  leaves the use in force.
- **Tools write the parts they can derive.** A part of a page or record that a
  tool can write from the tests, the matrix, or the question files
  (Split tests tables, the agreement sentence, `pending.md` row 1's
  list, the exercise rows' interpreter results) comes from a tool, and
  a check fails when it is stale.
- **Each front-end finding has a ledger row.** The row says whether a
  commit acted on the finding, a question holds it, or the text already
  decides it.
- **Each phase runs an exercise that writes programs.** A Write or Port
  session runs beside any Implement exercise.
- The choices of decisions 0051, 0054, 0055, and 0056 stay. The
  open-question aim of decision 0056 holds: the phase aims to end with
  fewer than 172 open questions, and its closing commit gives the
  change for each `**Waits for:**` kind.

Port suites, guides on the site, Diagnose batches beyond D-classes,
splitting the overloading principles (after I3 reports), an index of
what a test of validity can see, a shared impact scanner, a front-end
prototype of a done proposal, pair exercises, Propose X23 and X24,
ILP32 runs of the conversion tests, labels for the `unspecified` rules,
the library surface for tests, and rule-ID boundaries on the lowest
pages wait for a later phase. Later versions stay out of scope until
the maintainer decides `pending.md` row 7.
